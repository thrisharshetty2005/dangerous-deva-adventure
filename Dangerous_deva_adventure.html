How to Play

Move: Arrow Left/Right or A/D
Jump: Space, Up Arrow, or W — full air control, you can steer left/right mid‑air
Goal: reach the glowing flag on each level
Coins add score. Stars are bonus rewards worth more.
Spikes cost a life and send you back to the start of the current level only — never all the way to level 1.
Progress auto‑saves after every completed level, so you can quit and continue later.
You can move freely left and right — backtracking is always allowed.
